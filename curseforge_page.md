<p align="center"><strong>ReCraft Collect</strong> is a server-side community food collection mod for Minecraft Forge 1.20.1.<br>Players deposit food at a collection point and work together to reach a collective goal.</p>

<h3 align="center">✨ Key Features</h3>

<p align="center">
🍖 <strong>Community Collection</strong> — Players deposit food at a shared collection point at spawn.<br>
📊 <strong>Dynamic Boss Bar</strong> — Real-time progress bar visible to all players, with color changes at milestones.<br>
🏆 <strong>Leaderboards</strong> — Track top contributors with <code>/fc top</code>.<br>
🎯 <strong>Configurable Milestones</strong> — Set thresholds with custom messages and automatic server commands.<br>
⚙️ <strong>Custom Item Values</strong> — Override food values or make non-food items depositable.<br>
🖥️ <strong>100% Server-Side</strong> — No client-side installation required for players.
</p>

<br>

<h3 align="center">🎮 How It Works</h3>

<p align="center">
An admin sets a collection point block at spawn. Players interact with it:<br><br>
<strong>Right-click</strong> with food in hand → Deposits the held stack.<br>
<strong>Sneak + Right-click</strong> → Deposits <strong>all</strong> food from inventory.<br>
<strong>Right-click</strong> empty-handed → Shows personal and global score.<br><br>
Each food item is converted to units based on its <strong>nutritive value</strong> × quantity.<br>
The community works together to reach the season's goal (default: 1,000,000 units).
</p>

<br>

<h3 align="center">📊 Boss Bar Progression</h3>

<p align="center">
⬜ <strong>White</strong> — 0% to 49%<br>
🟦 <strong>Blue</strong> — 50% to 74%<br>
🟨 <strong>Yellow</strong> — 75% to 99%<br>
🟩 <strong>Green</strong> — 100% (Goal reached!)
</p>
